Self-evolving companion robot

A small, curious, playful wheeled companion.

Koda combines physical movement with high-level intelligence — an ESP32-S3 4WD platform, a BLE-connected Flutter app, LLM-driven vision, and graymatter persistent memory. Repurpose an old phone into an AI agent with embodied intelligence.

04 · Setup

Three steps to a companion.

Flash the firmware

Flash the files in koda_firmware/ to your ESP32-S3.

Run the app

flutter pub get then flutter run in koda_app/.

Connect

Power on the robot and pair via the app's BLE interface.
